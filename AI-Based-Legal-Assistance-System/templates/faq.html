⚖️ What is this system?

This system provides simple legal guidance, document checklists and information about common legal categories.

Required documents depend on the type of legal issue. You can check the Document Checklist section.

💻 What is Cyber Crime?

Cyber Crime includes online fraud, hacking, identity theft and other cyber-related offences.

👨‍⚖️ Does this replace a lawyer?

No. This system provides general legal information and guidance. For a specific legal matter, consult a qualified legal professional.

🏠 What is Property Law?

Property Law deals with ownership, registration, transfer and disputes related to property.

👨‍👩‍👧 What is Family Law?

Family Law covers matters such as marriage, divorce, child custody and maintenance.
